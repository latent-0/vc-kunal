I back founders early, and stay on to advise.

I invest at pre‑seed and seed, usually before there is much revenue and often before there is a finished product. I also sit with a small number of companies as a board advisor. I'm based in Bengaluru and work with founders in India, the rest of Asia, and Europe.

What I back

I look for founders who know a problem from the inside. Usually that means they worked in the industry or sold to the customer, or they built the first version because they needed it themselves.

Board advisory

Some founders want someone senior in the room without adding a full board seat. I take on a few of these roles at a time, sometimes in companies I've invested in and sometimes not.

In practice it means a regular meeting with the founders, being on hand between meetings, and helping with e.g. fundraising, first senior hires, board structure. If you're thinking about an advisor, email me with where the company is and what you need help with.

How I work

  1. I reply to every pitch within N days, including the ones I pass on.
  2. If I pass, I tell you why.
  3. After I invest, you can call me about hiring, customers and the next raise. I don't ask for a board seat.
