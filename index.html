Board advisory

Some founders want someone senior in the room without adding a full board seat. I take on a few of these roles at a time, sometimes in companies I've invested in and sometimes not.

In practice it means a regular meeting with the founders, being on hand between meetings, and helping with fundraising, early hiring and how the board runs. If you're thinking about an advisor, email me with where the company is and what you need help with.

How I work

  1. I reply to every pitch within a week, including the ones I pass on.
  2. If I pass, I tell you why.
  3. After I invest, you can call me about hiring, customers and the next raise. I don't ask for a board seat.

Send me a few lines about what you're building or where you need help.
